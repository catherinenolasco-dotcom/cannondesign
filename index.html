Case studies

Five projects, told straight

People Operations built from zero

Ridecell | Dec 2018 - Jan 2026

Challenge
Ridecell had no documentation beyond outdated files, headcount lived in spreadsheets, and employee questions had no tracked channel.

What I did
Built a 20+ procedure SOP library, ASKPeople (Jira and Confluence, tiered SLAs) and set up Sapling as the HR single source of truth, integrated with ADP and Zapier connections.

Result
Coverage grew from 3 to 5 countries with 6 consecutive clean SOC 2 Type 2 audits, approval cycles dropped from 1-2 days to 2-3 hours, and 5 to 10 tickets a day were resolved in 24 to 48 hours.

What I would do differently
I'd have started the SOP library and ticketing system in the first months. Documenting as I went would have been easier than catching up later.

One approval path for every HRIS change

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge
HRIS changes ran through an undocumented, unapproved process, which created audit risk.

What I did
Built a Claude-powered Employee Action Form requiring documentation and approval before any HRIS change, plus Notion workflows for new hire and offboarding.

Result
100% adoption within one to two months, an audit trail that had not existed, and preboarding within 24 hours of offer acceptance.

What I would do differently
I'd have piloted the form with one team first. Some people said it was too process-heavy, and early proof from a small group might have won them over faster.

  • Claude
  • Rippling
  • Notion
  • n8n

A reorganization with cost visibility

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge
A reorganization affecting 18 employees across the US and India needed severance and COBRA costs modeled and agreements drafted fast.

What I did
Built AI-enabled severance and cost modeling with Claude and Gemini that calculated severance, COBRA cost and COBRA months owed and drafted agreements, tracked on a shared spreadsheet for Payroll and IT.

Result
First-time cost visibility for a reduction in force, and agreement prep cut from 20 minutes per employee to about 3 hours total.

What I would do differently
I'd have built the cost model before the reorganization was decided. Leaders could then have compared scenarios rather than just seeing the cost of the chosen one.

  • Claude
  • Gemini
  • Google Sheets

What I would do first

A proposed plan built around the priorities in the role description.

Days 1 to 30

Listen and map

  • Meet HR, Finance, Payroll, Technology and the Centers of Excellence to hear what each needs from the HRIS.
  • Inventory the current HRIS, integrations and data flows.
  • Document employee lifecycle processes and where manual work and rework happen.
Days 31 to 60

Design the first moves

  • Draft requirements and evaluation criteria for the legacy system replacement with stakeholders.
  • Set HR data governance standards, controls and documentation.
  • Agree on the first dashboards leaders actually use.
Days 61 to 90

Ship and measure

  • Pilot one controlled change workflow with a small team before rolling it out.
  • Publish self-service resources and track support trends to cut manual work.
  • Share the first dashboards and a recommended replacement roadmap.
By month 6

The outcome

  • A clear path for the legacy system replacement, from evaluation through planning to implementation.
  • Documented data governance and controls the team follows.
  • Fewer repeat tickets and a smoother employee experience.

This is a starting point. I would adjust it after the first conversations with HR, Finance, Payroll and Technology leaders.
